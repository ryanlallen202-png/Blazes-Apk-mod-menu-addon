# DARK MATTER Soundboard

Create this folder on the headset:

`Android/data/com.TrassGames.Yeeps/files/mods/sounds/`

Put your own `.wav` or `.mp3` clips in it, then open **DARK MATTER → Soundboard → Scan Sounds Folder**.

Supported:
- WAV: PCM 16-bit, mono or stereo, 8–48 kHz
- MP3: Android MediaCodec-supported MP3 files
- Maximum clip length: 30 seconds

Press Play once to send the clip through your active Photon voice-chat recorder so players in the room can hear it from your voice stream. Only one clip plays at a time; looping and stacking are disabled. Playback uses the selected soundboard volume, and the original microphone source/settings are restored when the clip ends or you stop it.

If Photon voice chat is unavailable, the menu reports local-only playback instead of claiming the room can hear it.
