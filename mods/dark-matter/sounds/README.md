# DARK MATTER Soundboard

Place your own PCM 16-bit WAV clips in this folder on the Android device:

`Application.persistentDataPath/sounds/`

Open **DARK MATTER → Soundboard → Scan Sounds Folder**, then use Previous/Next and Play.

Supported files: WAV, PCM format 1, 16-bit, mono or stereo, 8–48 kHz, up to 30 seconds per clip.

Playback is local to your headset. This module does not inject audio into the microphone or transmit clips through voice chat.
